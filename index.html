About me

I’m Verno. August 2026 was when I first opened a terminal. With a little helper named Momo keeping me company, I typed git commit having no idea what it meant, and somehow that became day one.

Since then I’ve been fumbling through repos, breaking things, fixing them (mostly), and putting what I learn here. This isn’t a tutorial site — it’s my notebook. If you’re learning too, especially the messy early parts, I’d love to hear from you. Misery loves company, right?

“Every commit is a snapshot of a thought you almost forgot you had.”
